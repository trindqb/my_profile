About me

My name is Tri Nguyen Dang (Nguyễn Đăng Trị in Vietnamese).

I received the B.S. degree in Informatics Education from the University of Education, Hue University, Vietnam, in 2014, and the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Kyung Hee University, Seoul, South Korea, in 2023.

I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Kyung Hee University, Seoul, South Korea. I am currently Vice Dean of the Faculty of Information Technology and Director of the Center for Technology and AI Transformation at Gia Dinh University, Vietnam.

My research interests include network optimization, mobile cloud computing, multi-access edge computing, game theory, wireless communications, semantic communications, AR/VR applications for 5G and beyond, Explainable AI (XAI), Large Language Models (LLM), and AI Agentic systems.
